# Called It direction contract

Build path: code-led, derived from the user's explicit bold social receipts brief. No approved image comp or decision comp exists. DESIGN.md is this build's provisional seed, to be merged with the actual implementation after review.

## THESIS
The product makes a public claim accountable: freeze the words, oppose the stake, inspect the evidence, keep the receipt.

## OWN-WORLD
An ink-and-paper receipts desk for friends who argue about public announcements. Warm cream, dark ink, acid-yellow actions and restrained green/red outcomes. Condensed self-hosted Barlow lettering supplies the social confidence; DM Sans keeps rules and evidence readable. Ruled separators and clipped receipt corners carry the mechanism without illustrative decoration.

## STORY
The feed leads with real open takes. A receipt opens into the frozen proposition, exclusions, time window, hash, opposing stakes, sources and exact deciding quotation. A stamped verdict links to finalized transaction history. Profiles count only finalized matched settlements. Historical replays and recorded snapshots are explicit throughout.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
One concise product heading, a post action, the recorded/live provenance row and status/category filters. Two real future takes start the feed; a compact record rail shares the desktop. Mobile uses one content column and bottom navigation. The feed remains an operating product, with no marketing landing-page detour.

## FORM
Square paper surfaces, thin rules, large condensed take text, generous whitespace between groups, compact metadata within groups, and one prominent acid action. A receipt is the central object. State changes and public evidence outrank decoration. No invented balances, wins or verdicts.

## QUALITY BAR
The complete real claim must remain readable at 1440×960 and 390×844. Every action must expose its purpose, value and current state. Rules, evidence, verbatim quotes, callbacks and native claims must be discoverable. A rejected compilation must prevent staking. Live and historical material must be distinguishable without reading documentation. Review contrast, focus, mobile overflow, long identifiers, errors and empty states against the built screenshots. Preserve the own-world lettering and receipt hierarchy while fixing material defects.
