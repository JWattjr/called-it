# Called It
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified: Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, genlayer-js 1.1.8, four Python Intelligent Contracts on StudioNet, Vercel under wattxs-projects.
## Users
Friends taking opposite sides of public, resolvable claims; reviewers inspecting real receipts without a wallet.
## Product Purpose
Put GEN on your hot take. GenLayer validators decide who called it.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Compile a single proposition before staking. One counterparty. Even or bounded 1:4 odds. Immutable archived evidence; independent verdict and quote verification. Deterministic deadlines, defaults, refunds and pull claims. Public records only from finalized funded outcomes. Explicitly labelled historical replays. Politics, harm, private individuals, health and asset prices are excluded. Owner approval is required before demo deposits.
## Brand Commitments
Bold social receipts: take cards, large opposing-side action, stamped CALLED IT / MISSED / VOID verdicts. An application feed rather than a generic landing page.
## Evidence on Hand
Reference projects are implementation references only. No Called It deployment or outcome exists at initialization; never manufacture one.
