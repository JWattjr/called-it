**0:00–0:20 — The feed.** Open Called It without a wallet. “A take, a stake, a receipt. GenLayer compiles the rule before money moves, then judges the evidence.” Point out the live takes and their real future deadlines. The data provenance row distinguishes recorded proof from a fresh finalized read.

**0:20–0:45 — Official YES.** Open the historical Apple Vision Pro take. Point out the historical replay label, past as-of time, deadline and rule hash. Expand the receipt. Follow the immutable official Apple announcement and read the deciding quote. Show the finalized YES and its transaction.

**0:45–1:05 — Reported is different.** Open the Nintendo replay. Its rule requires the official retail-name reveal. The permanent pre-reveal Wikipedia revision describes an unnamed console and reporting. Inspect the actual NO ruling and quote. Do not substitute an assumed result if this live run has not finalized.

**1:05–1:20 — Defaults and rejection.** Open the no-evidence take: code applied its frozen default, explicitly labelled. Then open the subjective “flop” claim and show the compiler's rejection before any stake.

Also open the Grammy scheduling replay: the revision-5 v2 judge returns VOID_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE because the captured article schedules the event without establishing completion. The old revision-4 disagreement stays archived and its original refund job remains separate.

**1:20–1:45 — Money and record.** On the funded replay, show the different counterparty address, both tiny stakes, settlement, winner's claim and exact native child transfer. Open the winner's profile to show the finalized record update. Unfunded takes do not count.

**1:45–2:00 — Verify it yourself.** Open The receipts. Follow a canonical contract explorer page and a transaction page. Show `deploy/proof.json` and the read-only verifier. “The model judges the announcement. Code counts the GEN. The receipt is public.”

Only demonstrate states present in the release manifest. If a required transaction, payout or browser signing flow remains blocked, disclose it instead of calling the submission ready.
